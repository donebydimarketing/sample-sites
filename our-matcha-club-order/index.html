About the owner

Meet Christina. She did it scared.

C

Christina

Founder, Our Matcha Club
Small cafeMatchaArt
Sometimes the best things happen when you do them scared.
  1. 1

    Coffee first

    Christina moved to the States to be with family. She brought her love for coffee to a job as a barista.

  2. 2

    Back home

    She came home and worked in the corporate world. She is a graphic artist by profession.

  3. 3

    A coffee side hustle

    Her passion for coffee led to bottled cold brew and a sandwich. She sold them at pop-up booths at local events and festivals.

  4. 4

    Matcha took over

    Coffee pop-ups were everywhere, but nobody in her neighborhood served just matcha. So she switched to matcha, and Our Matcha Club was born. She was still working full time.

  5. 5

    Version 1, 2 and 3

    The pop-up kept changing as the club grew. Each version taught her what people love.

  6. 6

    A home for the club

    After years in corporate, she quit her job to build a permanent home for Our Matcha Club. A little green corner for a peaceful cup of no drama, just good good matcha.
