SAMPLE PITCH SITE (scroll through the shop)

1. Test it: open index.html in a browser. Scroll to walk through the placeholder video.
2. Swap in a real video:
   - Film 10 to 20 seconds, phone sideways, walking slowly toward the shop door.
   - Run: ./make_frames.sh your-video.mp4   (needs ffmpeg)
   - Open index.html and change TOTAL = 90 to the number of frames made.
3. Change the text: shop name, services, prices, address, hours, phone, Book now link.
4. Put it on GitHub:
   - Create a new public repo on github.com.
   - Upload index.html and the frames folder (drag and drop).
   - Settings > Pages > Deploy from a branch > main > Save.
   - Your link shows up after about a minute.
